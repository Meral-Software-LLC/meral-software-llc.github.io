About

Point the camera at your cube and OpenCube reads it, shows a short solution, and teaches Beginner, CFOP and Roux step by step. A solve timer with scrambles, inspection and per-method statistics rounds it out. Seven languages, no internet required.

  • Reads all six faces through the camera
  • Short solutions from a two-phase solver
  • Beginner, CFOP and Roux lessons with 3D animations
  • Timer with scrambles, inspection and per-method stats
