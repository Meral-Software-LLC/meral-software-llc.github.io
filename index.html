How we build

The same four rules in every app.

On device, always

Camera, LiDAR and motion data are processed on the phone or watch and never uploaded. Every app works in airplane mode.

No accounts

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to remember. Open the app and use it.

Pay once

Each app is free to try and has one purchase that unlocks it forever. No subscriptions, no ads, no tip jars.

Native to Apple platforms

Built with Swift and SwiftUI, using the sensors and frameworks each device already has. Fast, small and at home on your device.
