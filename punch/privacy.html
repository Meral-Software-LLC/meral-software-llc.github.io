Privacy Policy for Punch

Last updated September 20, 2026

Punch collects no data. Not usage analytics, not crash reports, not an email address, nothing. There are no accounts to create and no servers to talk to. The app works entirely on your device and works without an internet connection.

What stays on your device

Anything the app saves, such as your history, settings or scans, is stored only in the app's own storage on your device and in your device backups if you have those turned on. You can delete it at any time by deleting the app.

Device permissions

  • Motion sensors — read on the watch during a session.
  • Health — Punch can record workouts to the Health app on your device if you allow it. Health data is never read for any other purpose and never leaves your device.

Purchases

A one-time purchase unlocks the full app. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, and we receive no personal information about you from a purchase.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code, advertising, or tracking of any kind. It does not send data to anyone, including us.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Since the app cannot contact us, the app itself cannot notify you.
