About

OpenPack measures an object and a space with the LiDAR scanner, then tells you whether one fits in the other, with a 3D preview you can turn.

  • Scan any object into a measured 3D shape
  • Scan a trunk, bin, doorway or shelf
  • A clear yes or no, with the preview to prove it
