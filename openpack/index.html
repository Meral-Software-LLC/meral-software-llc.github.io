OpenPack

Will it fit? Scan the object and the space and find out.

OpenPack uses the LiDAR scanner to measure an object and a space, then tells you whether one fits in the other with a 3D preview.

  • Scan any object into a measured 3D shape
  • Scan a trunk, bin, doorway or shelf
  • Yes/no fit verdict with a 3D preview
